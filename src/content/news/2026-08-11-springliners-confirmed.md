We're delighted to confirm that **The Springliners** will be opening Blewbury Beer Festival 2026.

**Jo and Matt Napper** will get the music underway from **2:00pm until 3:00pm**, bringing their own distinctive **Blewbury take on bluegrass** to start the afternoon.

Whether you're arriving for your first pint, collecting your festival glass or simply settling in for an afternoon of great music, The Springliners will provide the perfect soundtrack to the festival opening.

The live music programme continues throughout the afternoon with **Blewbury Brass Band**, **Dave Whitlock**, and **The Lords of Mischief**, taking us right through to festival close.

Visit our **Music** page to see the full entertainment programme, with more festival updates to follow over the coming weeks.
