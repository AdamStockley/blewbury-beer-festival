There are now **less than two weeks to go** until Blewbury Beer Festival returns to Blewbury Village Hall on **Saturday 3 October**—and excitement is building!

The drinks are being lined up, the entertainment is confirmed and preparations are well underway for a fantastic afternoon and evening of great beer, food and live music.

We're also delighted to welcome our latest barrel sponsors:

- **Heads Up Theatre Group**
- **SK Maintenance**
- **Tom Savage Roofing & Building**
- **Parker & Warne Vehicle Services**
- **Thomas Merrifield**
- **Volonte Group Ltd**
- **Larkmead Vets**

Their generous support helps make the festival possible and means we can raise even more money for local good causes. A huge thank you to them—and to all the businesses already supporting this year's event.

We can't wait to welcome everyone on 3 October. See you there!
