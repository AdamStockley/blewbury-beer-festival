We're delighted to announce that **six local businesses** have already confirmed their support for **Blewbury Beer Festival 2026** by sponsoring one of this year's beer and cider barrels.

Barrel sponsorship is a great way for local businesses to support the festival, gain exposure on our website and at the event, and help us continue raising money for local good causes.

We're very grateful to all of our confirmed sponsors for helping make this year's festival possible.

There are still sponsorship opportunities available, so if your business would like to get involved, we'd love to hear from you.
