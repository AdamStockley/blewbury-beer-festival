# Blewbury Beer Festival
## Content Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines what content belongs on each page.

The objective is to avoid duplication and keep the homepage focused.

---

# Homepage

Purpose:

Generate excitement.

Answer four questions:

- What?
- When?
- Where?
- Why?

Include:

- Hero
- Countdown
- Beer / Music / Food summary
- Good Causes summary
- Latest news
- Footer

Do NOT include:

- Complete beer lists
- Entertainment schedules
- FAQs
- Parking details
- Sponsor lists
- Long-form text

---

# Beer

Purpose:

Everything relating to drinks.

Include:

- Breweries
- Beer list
- Ciders
- Tasting notes
- ABV
- Pump clips (where available)

---

# Music

Purpose:

Entertainment.

Include:

- Artists
- Timetable
- Performer biographies
- Links

---

# Food

Purpose:

Food vendors.

Include:

- Menus
- Dietary information
- Opening times

---

# Visit

Purpose:

Everything required to attend.

Include:

- Maps
- Parking
- Public transport
- Accessibility
- Payment
- FAQs
- Opening times

---

# Good Causes

Purpose:

Explain why the festival exists.

Include:

- Beneficiaries
- Previous fundraising
- Community impact

---

# Volunteer

Purpose:

Recruit helpers.

Include:

- Roles
- Times
- Contact details
- Benefits

---

# News

Purpose:

Announcements only.

Examples:

- Festival confirmed
- First breweries announced
- Entertainment announced
- Food vendors announced
- Volunteer recruitment
- Sponsors

Avoid duplicating static information already found elsewhere.

---

# Sponsors

Purpose:

Recognise supporters.

Include:

- Logos
- Links
- Sponsor information

---

# Footer

Always include:

- Festival name
- Date
- Location
- Good Causes statement
- Contact
- Copyright

---

# Content Style

Use:

Short paragraphs.

Meaningful headings.

Simple language.

Write for visitors, not search engines.

---

# Review Process

Before publishing new content ask:

1. Does it belong on this page?
2. Is it already explained elsewhere?
3. Is it still current?
4. Does it support the festival?

If the answer to any of these is "No", reconsider whether it should be published.
